SECTION 1 — CAMPAIGN TYPES
================================================================================

PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGN
A promotional campaign targets a broad audience with time-limited offers,
discounts, or seasonal deals. The primary goal is to increase sales volume
in a short period. Promotional campaigns work best for Young Professionals
and Premium Shoppers who are actively looking for deals.
Typical open rate: 25-35%.
Typical conversion rate: 2-4%.
Best channels: email, social, web.
Best segments: Young Professional, Premium Shopper.
Budget tip: Allocate 40% to email, 40% to social, 20% to web for best ROI.
When to use: Product launches, seasonal sales, flash sales, clearance events.
Risk: High unsubscribe rate if sent too frequently. Limit to once per week.

RETENTION CAMPAIGN
A retention campaign targets existing high-value customers with the goal of
preventing churn and deepening loyalty. These customers already know the brand
and respond to personalised, exclusive offers rather than generic discounts.
Typical open rate: 55-70%.
Typical conversion rate: 10-20%.
Best channels: email, direct.
Best segments: Senior Professional, Premium Shopper, VIP customers.
Budget tip: Direct channel has highest conversion (40%+) but is expensive.
Use email for volume, direct for top VIP customers only.
When to use: When high-value customers show reduced activity, after major purchase,
anniversary campaigns, loyalty reward programs.
Risk: Low risk of churn if done right. Over-contacting can feel intrusive.

LIFECYCLE CAMPAIGN
A lifecycle campaign nurtures new customers through an automated onboarding
sequence. It educates them about the product, builds trust, and guides them
toward their first purchase. Typically delivered as a drip email sequence
over 7-14 days.
Typical open rate: 50-65%.
Typical conversion rate: 5-8%.
Best channels: email.
Best segments: Student, new customers, first-time buyers.
Budget tip: Low cost per send since it is automated. Invest in content quality.
When to use: Immediately after a new customer registers or makes first purchase.
Risk: Poor onboarding leads to early churn. Personalisation is critical.

REENGAGEMENT CAMPAIGN
A reengagement campaign targets customers who have been inactive for 60 or
more days. The goal is reactivation — bringing them back before they churn
permanently. These campaigns need a strong incentive since the customer has
already disengaged.
Typical open rate: 15-25%.
Typical conversion rate: 0.5-2%.
Best channels: email, SMS.
Best segments: Young Professional, Student — customers who were once active.
Budget tip: Keep spend low — reengagement has low ROI. If open rate stays
below 10% after two attempts, remove from active lists.
When to use: After 60 days of inactivity. One attempt at 60 days, one at 90 days.
Risk: High unsubscribe and spam complaint rate. Use sparingly with strong offers.

AWARENESS CAMPAIGN
An awareness campaign introduces the brand or a new product to a broad audience.
The primary goal is reach and impressions rather than immediate conversion.
ROI is measured in brand recall and top-of-funnel growth rather than direct sales.
Typical click rate: 3-6%.
Typical conversion rate: 0.3-0.5%.
Best channels: social, web.
Best segments: All segments — broad reach is the goal.
Budget tip: Social gives highest reach per rupee for awareness. Web retargeting
converts awareness into consideration.
When to use: New product launches, entering new markets, rebranding.
Risk: Hard to measure direct ROI. Set reach and impression targets, not sales targets.

SECTION 2 — MARKETING KPI DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

OPEN RATE
Percentage of delivered emails that were opened by the recipient.
Formula: (Emails Opened / Emails Delivered) x 100
Industry benchmark: 20-25% for B2C marketing.
Platform benchmark: 36% (email channel average across all campaigns).
Good: Above 35%. Average: 20-35%. Poor: Below 20%.
Action if poor: Improve subject lines, test send times, clean email list.

CLICK RATE (CTR)
Percentage of delivered emails where a recipient clicked at least one link.
Formula: (Emails Clicked / Emails Delivered) x 100
Industry benchmark: 2-5%.
Platform benchmark: 9% (email channel average).
Good: Above 8%. Average: 3-8%. Poor: Below 3%.
Action if poor: Improve call-to-action, reduce number of links, personalise content.

CONVERSION RATE
Percentage of recipients who completed the desired action (purchase, signup, etc).
Formula: (Conversions / Total Reached) x 100
Benchmark varies by channel:
  Email: 2-6% good, above 10% excellent.
  Social: 0.3-1% good.
  Web: 2-4% good.
  Direct: 20-45% good (high intent audience).
Platform benchmark: Email 2.7-17.5% depending on campaign type.

COST PER CONVERSION
Total campaign spend divided by number of conversions.
Formula: Total Spend / Total Conversions
Lower is better. Target varies by product margin.
Platform benchmark: ₹10-₹90 depending on channel.
Direct channel: ₹12 per conversion (most efficient).
Social channel: ₹42 per conversion.
Reengagement email: ₹89 per conversion (least efficient).

REACH EFFICIENCY
Percentage of target reach actually achieved.
Formula: (Actual Reach / Target Reach) x 100
Good: Above 85%. Average: 70-85%. Poor: Below 70%.
Platform benchmark: 68-112% across campaigns.

BUDGET UTILISATION
Percentage of campaign budget spent so far.
Formula: (Spent / Total Budget) x 100
Compare against percentage of campaign time elapsed.
If budget utilisation significantly exceeds time elapsed — spending too fast.
If budget utilisation significantly lags time elapsed — underspending.
Ideal: Budget utilisation should track within 10% of time elapsed.

LIFETIME VALUE (LTV)
Total revenue a customer is expected to generate over their relationship
with the brand.
Platform average LTV: ₹24,537.
Premium Shopper average LTV: ₹49,000 (highest).
Student average LTV: ₹2,800 (lowest).
Use LTV to prioritise which segments deserve higher campaign spend.

UNSUBSCRIBE RATE
Percentage of recipients who unsubscribe after receiving a campaign.
Formula: (Unsubscribes / Emails Delivered) x 100
Acceptable: Below 0.3%. Warning: 0.3-0.5%. Critical: Above 0.5%.
High unsubscribe rate signals: wrong audience, wrong frequency, poor content.

SECTION 3 — CHANNEL BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================

EMAIL CHANNEL
Best for: Retention, lifecycle, reengagement campaigns.
Highest ROI channel overall. Personalisation increases open rates by 26%.
Best send times: Tuesday to Thursday, 9am-11am or 2pm-4pm.
Subject line best practices:
  — Keep under 50 characters.
  — Use the customer name in subject line.
  — Create urgency without being clickbait.
  — Avoid spam trigger words (FREE, URGENT, ACT NOW).
Frequency: Maximum 2 emails per week per customer. More causes fatigue.
Personalisation: Use customer name, reference past purchases, segment-specific offers.
Platform performance: 36% open rate, 9% click rate across all campaigns.

SOCIAL CHANNEL
Best for: Awareness, promotional campaigns. High reach, lower conversion.
Best for Young Professional and Student segments who are social-active.
Platform performance: 4-6% click rate, 0.28-0.42% conversion rate.
Best use: Top of funnel — drive awareness and traffic, convert via email/web retargeting.
Budget allocation: Use social for reach, email for conversion.

DIRECT CHANNEL
Best for: VIP and high-value retention campaigns.
Highest conversion rate (43.2%) but most expensive per send.
Reserve exclusively for Senior Professional and Premium Shopper segments.
Platform performance: 90% open rate, 72% click rate, 43.2% conversion rate.
Never use for broad audiences — cost per send is too high.

WEB CHANNEL
Best for: Awareness and promotional campaigns driving site traffic.
Works best paired with email or social (retargeting).
Platform performance: 3% conversion rate, 38% bounce rate average.
Reduce bounce rate by: matching landing page to ad message, fast load times,
clear call to action above the fold.

SMS CHANNEL
Best for: Urgent promotions, reengagement, time-sensitive offers.
Highest open rate of all channels (98%) but intrusive if overused.
Best for Indian market — WhatsApp and SMS have high engagement.
Keep messages under 160 characters. Include opt-out option.
Frequency: Maximum 2 SMS per month per customer.
Never send between 9pm and 8am.

SECTION 4 — AUDIENCE TARGETING
================================================================================

ML-BASED TARGETING
The platform uses a Random Forest machine learning model to score each customer
on their likelihood to respond to each campaign type. Scores range from 0 to 1.
A score of 0.7 or above indicates high likelihood of response.
The model considers: customer segment, preferred channel, lifetime value,
total purchases, and age.
Auto-select feature automatically identifies customers above confidence threshold.
Recommended minimum confidence threshold: 0.5 for most campaigns.
For high-value campaigns (retention, VIP): use 0.7 threshold.

CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
Young Professional: Age 22-35, moderate spenders, tech-savvy, social and email active.
  Average LTV: ₹10,500. Responds to: promotional, reengagement campaigns.

Premium Shopper: High purchase frequency, brand loyal, high LTV.
  Average LTV: ₹49,000. Responds to: retention, awareness campaigns.

Student: Price-sensitive, new customers, low LTV, deal-seekers.
  Average LTV: ₹2,800. Responds to: lifecycle, promotional campaigns with discounts.

Senior Professional: Highest LTV, low churn risk, prefers email and direct.
  Average LTV: ₹78,500. Responds to: retention, VIP loyalty campaigns.

AUDIENCE SELECTION BEST PRACTICES
Always use ML auto-select as a starting point — it is data-driven.
Review auto-selected audience before confirming — check segment distribution.
Avoid targeting the same customer with more than 2 campaigns simultaneously.
For reengagement — only target customers inactive for 60-90 days, not longer.
For retention — prioritise customers with LTV above ₹30,000.
For promotional — broader audience is fine, prioritise email-active customers.

SECTION 5 — BUDGET MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

BUDGET ALLOCATION BY CHANNEL
Based on platform performance data, recommended budget allocation:
  Email: 40% of budget — highest ROI, lowest cost per conversion.
  Direct: 25% of budget — highest conversion rate, use for VIP only.
  Web: 20% of budget — good for promotional and awareness.
  Social: 15% of budget — best for reach and awareness, lower conversion.
  SMS: Supplement email, not replace — use for urgency and reengagement.

BUDGET PACING
Monitor budget utilisation vs time elapsed weekly.
If budget utilisation is more than 15% ahead of time elapsed — slow down spend.
If budget utilisation is more than 15% behind time elapsed — increase frequency.
Never exhaust budget before campaign end date — leave 10% for last-week push.

ROI BY CAMPAIGN TYPE (Platform Data)
Retention: Highest ROI — VIP Loyalty generated ₹2,63,500 on ₹18,500 spend (14x).
Promotional: Good ROI — Summer Sale generated ₹6,47,500 on ₹32,000 spend (20x).
Lifecycle: Moderate ROI — good for long-term LTV but low immediate revenue.
Reengagement: Lowest ROI — high cost per conversion, use sparingly.
Awareness: Difficult to measure direct ROI — track reach and brand metrics.

COST BENCHMARKS (Platform Data)
Cost per email send: approximately ₹2.
Cost per SMS send: approximately ₹0.50 (Fast2SMS).
Cost per direct mail: approximately ₹15-25.
Cost per social impression: approximately ₹0.50-1.

SECTION 6 — PLATFORM SPECIFIC GUIDANCE
================================================================================

CAMPAIGN CREATION WORKFLOW
1. Define campaign type and goal clearly before creating.
2. Set realistic reach targets based on available audience size.
3. Use ML auto-select for audience — review before confirming.
4. Generate AI content — always personalise with segment-specific tone.
5. Review content before sending — AI output should be edited, not sent raw.
6. Send immediately for time-sensitive campaigns, schedule for planned ones.
7. Monitor performance after 24 hours — check open rate and click rate.
8. Use Campaign Intelligence Engine to get AI recommendations mid-campaign.

CAMPAIGN INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
The platform provides per-campaign AI analysis answering:
  — Am I doing it right?
  — What is working and what is not?
  — What is the best I can do with remaining budget?
  — Will I hit my goal?
Intelligence reports are generated fresh daily and cached for performance.
Use refresh=true parameter to force a new analysis.

WHEN TO PAUSE A CAMPAIGN
Pause if open rate drops below 10% for email campaigns.
Pause if unsubscribe rate exceeds 0.5%.
Pause if budget utilisation exceeds 90% with more than 20% time remaining.
Pause if reach efficiency is below 50% after 25% of campaign duration.

MULTI-CHANNEL CAMPAIGNS
When running email + SMS together:
  Send email first — higher engagement, lower cost.
  Send SMS 2-3 days later to non-openers only.
  This maximises reach while controlling cost.

AI CONTENT GENERATION
Platform generates email subject lines and body copy using Groq AI.
Always specify: campaign type, target segment, and tone.
Available tones: professional, energetic, empathetic, urgent.
AI content uses placeholder for customer name — system replaces at send time.
Edit AI output before sending — treat it as a first draft, not final copy.
